# ActiveTrust — No-Mandatory-APC IEEE Journal Shortlist

## Important interpretation

The recommendations below mean **Traditional submission with no open-access APC**, not necessarily zero publication cost. Mandatory overlength/page charges, color charges, or other publication charges may still apply. Authors should verify the target’s current author page immediately before submission.

| Rank | Journal | Fit for current ActiveTrust topic | Current quartile indicator | Traditional-route fee evidence | Submission positioning |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)** | Strong if the paper emphasizes gateway-managed secure routing, network operation, route repair, resource management, and measurable service reliability | SCImago displays SJR 2025 = 1.389, Q1 | Official TNSM policy states Traditional submission requires no OA payment; up to 10 pages are free, with mandatory US$220 per page beyond 10 up to 16 pages | Best no-mandatory-APC fit after the paper is reframed as network/service management and the experimental evidence is completed |

| 3 | **IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS)** | Potentially strong for security, but only after rigorous threat modeling, formal security analysis, and reproducibility | Quartile should be verified in the institution’s required database before submission | Official SPS page confirms security scope and encourages code/data release; current author-fee policy must be checked from the linked author-information document | Use only if the paper is rewritten as a security-method paper rather than primarily a routing simulation |
| 4 | **IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC)** | Strong conceptual fit for secure and dependable systems, but highly selective | Verify current quartile in the required database | Official author-information document must be checked directly for current page/overlength charges; IEEE Xplore PDF was rate-limited in this audit | Ambitious target after formal security/correctness and reproducibility upgrades |

## Recommended first submission

The recommended first target is **IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management** under Traditional submission, provided that the authors complete the missing implementation and results evidence. The current manuscript should be positioned around the operational problem of secure, energy-aware routing managed by a trusted base station. The paper must fit within the journal’s free 10-page two-column allowance or obtain author approval for the mandatory overlength charges.

The alternative is **IEEE Internet of Things Journal** under Traditional submission. It is an excellent scope fit for sensor-network security and IoT protocols, but the manuscript should be condensed, and the authors should be prepared for the mandatory charge beyond eight published pages.

## Submission-cost checklist

Before selecting the final venue, confirm the following directly on the journal’s author page:

| Cost item | Must be checked |
|---|---:|
| Traditional submission has no OA APC | Yes |
| Mandatory overlength/page charge | Yes |
| Color-in-print charge | Yes |
| Supplementary-material charge | Yes |
| Institutional transformative agreement | Optional |
| Low-income-country APC discount | Optional; relevant only if OA is selected |
| Current page limit and article type | Yes |
| Current template and submission portal | Yes |
